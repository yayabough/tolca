**User:** We're about to sign with Pomerleau for a renovation project in Québec. Are they clear?

**Agent calls** `qc_contractor_check` (free MCP tool) or `qc-contractor-check` over x402 ($0.10).

**Tolca answers (live check, Nov 1, 2026):**

- Verdict: **allow**, risk **10**
- RBQ licence 2743-1162-70: **active**
- RENA (public-contract ban): **not listed**
- Environmental penalties: **none found** under this name
- SEAO: **28 public contracts** awarded in the last 12 months
- Sanctions (US, UK, EU, Canada): **clear**

**Agent replies:** Pomerleau holds an active RBQ licence and shows no ban or penalty in the sources checked. Before signing, confirm that the licence subcategories cover the planned work and that the company name on the contract matches the licence holder.
